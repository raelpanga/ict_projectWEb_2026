what is Salesforce CRM

Salesforce is a cloud-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform that helps businesses manage customer data, sales processes, service requests, and marketing campaigns in one system. Instead of storing information in spreadsheets or separate tools, companies use Salesforce to keep everything organized in a single place.

Because Salesforce runs in the cloud, users can access it from anywhere with an internet connection. This makes it powerful for modern businesses that need flexibility and real-time updates.


Learn more about Salesforce on the Official Salesforce Website.

Salesforce overview

What is salesforce used for

Companies use Salesforce to track leads, manage customer accounts, create opportunities, and monitor sales performance. It also allows businesses to automate processes using tools like Flow and validation rules.

Beyond sales, Salesforce can support customer service teams, marketing departments, and even internal operations. Its flexibility makes it useful for organizations of all sizes.

Why Salesforce is Important

Salesforce is one of the most in-demand platforms in the tech industry. Many companies look for professionals who understand how to configure and customize Salesforce. Learning Salesforce opens doors to careers such as Salesforce Administrator, Developer, Consultant, and Business Analyst.

What makes Salesforce powerful is its low-code and no-code capabilities. Even without advanced programming knowledge, users can build automation, apps, and reports inside the system.

  • Used by companies of all sizes
  • Strong demand for Salesforce professionals
  • Low-code and no-code development options
  • Highly customizable and scalable
